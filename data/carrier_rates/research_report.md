# Malaysian carrier tariff research — 8 November 2026

**Result: no complete verified current domestic rate card was obtained.** The production `rate_cards.csv`, `pricing_rules.csv`, and `postcode_zones.csv` are header-only. None of the seven requested routes can be priced defensibly by a local tariff calculator from this research. Six monetary observations in `source_evidence.csv` are partial official public references, **not** complete shipment rates. They must not be imported as production tariffs.

Fourteen providers or platforms were assessed. `carriers.csv` and `missing_rate_cards.csv` give provider-by-provider sources and gaps; `coverage_matrix.csv` covers the seven route classes. All fourteen need manual interaction or account access to obtain complete applicable rates. Direct provider fetches from this environment previously returned proxy HTTP 403, so this pass used indexed official pages. No CAPTCHA, login or API was bypassed.

## Official findings and limits

- [J&T Express](https://www.jtexpress.my/index.php/shipping-rates), [City-Link](https://www.citylinkexpress.com/calculator/), and [Skynet](https://skynet.com.my/tariff-calculator) offer rate calculators. City-Link's [terms](https://www.citylinkexpress.com/terms-conditions/) say estimates may differ from actual charges and use the greater of physical and volumetric weight. A complete zone and band schedule was not obtained.
- [Ninja Van](https://www.ninjavan.co/en-my/support/shipper-support/pricing-and-coverage/how-to-calculate-and-check-shipping-rates) directs shippers to a price estimate or account rate card. [Pos Malaysia's API documentation](https://api-doc.pos.com.my/) includes a rate calculator for authorized contract customers; no credentials were used. [SPX terms](https://spx.com.my/en/terms-of-service.html) place rates on its platform or portal.
- [DHL eCommerce](https://www.dhl.com/my-en/home/ecommerce/request-a-quote.html) directs businesses to request a quote. [Aramex Malaysia](https://www.aramex.com/my/en/services-solutions/service-rate-guide) links a service and rate guide, but its current domestic applicability, full bands and ancillary rules were not verified. Its [signature surcharge](https://www.aramex.com/my/en/legal-details/signature-surcharge) is an optional component only.
- [EasyParcel](https://easyparcel.com/my/shipping-rates/) displays starting marketplace prices for several carriers, including Pos MELPlus, J&T and DHL eCommerce. These are neither complete tariffs nor carrier-direct prices. [Lalamove](https://www.lalamove.com/en-my/all-vehicle-pricing-detail) publishes an additional-stop component, but a full route-specific vehicle quote remains necessary. [TheLorry](https://thelorry.com/my/business/faq2/) describes distance, vehicle and additional-stop pricing without a complete public matrix.
- [City-Link's emergency fuel notice](https://www.citylinkexpress.com/emergency-fuel-surcharge-efs-notice/) provides a route-specific surcharge component. It does not supply the base rate. The notice's tax wording is recorded as a source condition only; **no tax rule** was entered because current applicability to a complete tariff was not independently confirmed with authoritative tax guidance.
- No verified official public domestic tariff was located for Best Express, GDEX or Lazada Express in this pass. Their coverage and pricing remain unknown.

## Next collection step

For each provider, obtain an authorized current tariff PDF or export with effective dates, exact zone/postcode mapping, weight bands, volumetric divisor, rounding, service restrictions, taxes and all surcharges. Validate non-overlapping bands and dates before populating production CSVs. For dynamic providers, prefer an authorized rate API or manual quote. Keep aggregator, coupon, contract and public prices in separate provenance classes. Request exact route inputs and retain dated evidence; do not infer postcodes from city names.

A local calculator is appropriate only for a complete versioned card, such as a verified current published or contracted schedule. Account-specific and dynamic services require authorized live or manual quotes. This branch contains research data only; the shipping engine, agent and simulation were not changed.
