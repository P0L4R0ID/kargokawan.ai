# Festival Idea Putrajaya · five-minute KargoKawan walkthrough

This is a **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION — NOT REAL CARRIER PRICES, QUOTES OR BOOKINGS**. It needs no CSV, courier login or API key. The five-order scenario lives only in the current Streamlit session; it never writes to private PostgreSQL jobs or quote evidence.

1. **0:00–0:45 · Start.** Open the app. **Five-order walkthrough** is the first workspace tab. The KargoKawan operations assistant shows one next action: **Load five-order fictional scenario**. Click it. Five example orders now appear under **Delivery orders**. They start at postcode 50000; three go to distinct Klang Valley stops and two go to George Town and Johor Bahru. The cards show postcode, parcel size, weight and deadline. The existing intake validator checks the underlying order records.
2. **0:45–1:45 · Compare.** Open **Plan & compare**. The deterministic exact-cover optimiser selects one three-stop vehicle for FEST001–003 and separate parcels for FEST004–005. Its computed assumed cost is **RM113**, compared with an eligible all-parcel **RM132** baseline, a **RM19** fictional difference. Show the rejected slow parcel options and their deadline reasons. Open **Assumption evidence and all alternatives** only if judges ask for quote IDs, route stops, source labels or solver details. These amounts come from fictional quote assumptions and are calculated by the solver, not written into the page as a recommendation.
3. **1:45–2:45 · Review and decide.** Return to **Overview** and click **Preview five-order fictional plan**. Open **Review & approve** to show the exact shipment groups, assumed total and proposal-only notice. The manifest is unavailable. In **Review & approve**, explicitly click **Approve five-order fictional plan**, or discard the proposal there. Approval rechecks orders, quote evidence and availability. It authorizes fictional preparation only.
4. **2:45–3:30 · Prepare.** Open **Review & approve** and click **Download fictional preparation manifest — NOT A BOOKING**. Its five rows identify the fictional recipients, postcodes, selected method and assumption IDs. Return to **Overview** and click **Mark fictional packages prepared**. This is an in-session preparation marker, not dispatch or delivery.
5. **3:30–4:30 · Respond to disruption.** In **Activity**, click **Simulate fictional van disruption**. The approval and preparation markers clear. **Overview** explains **What changed**: the recalculated all-parcel option costs **RM132** under the fictional assumptions, **RM19** more than the prior plan. A new preview and explicit approval are required. **Activity** records the suggested plan, human decision and disruption separately. **Restore fictional van** is available here too.
6. **4:30–5:00 · Show a blocker.** In **Delivery orders**, choose FEST004, set **Revised fictional deadline (days)** to 1 and click **Apply fictional deadline change**. **Plan & compare** identifies FEST004 as uncovered, and **Overview** shows **Blocked**. No proposal or approved manifest can be produced, and no parcel baseline is displayed without eligible coverage.

The **Private Pilot** workspace remains separate, requiring Google OIDC and the owner-scoped Render/Supabase backend. Its manual quote drafts retain provenance restrictions; EasyParcel Open API OAuth is disabled. **Advanced & other demos** contains the original simulated dashboard, four-order demo and imported-quotation tools. The redesign changes presentation and navigation, not their business logic or APIs.

The operations assistant reads the existing validator, quotation review, deterministic solver, approval flags, provider availability and session events. It does not use an LLM to invent rates, guarantee arrival, book a carrier or claim real savings. A solver-limited feasible result is not described as globally optimal. The demo does not contact carriers. Session data disappears when the browser session ends.
