# Festival Idea Putrajaya · five-minute orders-first demo

The public workspace starts with **five fictional deliveries already loaded**. Prices and orders are fictional; there are no real bookings, carrier calls or pilot database writes. No login or CSV is needed.

1. **0:00–0:50 · Inspect the orders.** Open the app. Under **KargoKawan**, the Orders step says **You have 5 deliveries to arrange**. The pickup, destination cities, postcodes, weights, dimensions and deadlines come from the existing validated scenario. At this point there is **no selected delivery method or plan cost**. The detailed order evidence and deadline control are expandable.
2. **0:50–1:45 · Request planning.** Click **Find the best delivery plan**. This is the first time the fictional mixed-method optimiser runs. The Plan step leads with the assumed total and all-courier comparison, then presents the selected three-stop van first and separate courier parcels for FEST004–005. Its calculated assumed total is **RM113**, with a valid **RM132** all-courier comparison and **RM19** difference. These figures are derived from the fictional quote dataset and solver, never real rates or guaranteed savings. Open **How this was calculated** for quote IDs, rejected options, sources and solver status.
3. **1:45–2:40 · Review before approval.** Click **Review this delivery plan**. The Approve step shows a concise decision amount, order coverage and group count; full groups are available only in **View full plan details**. The manifest is still unavailable. Click **Approve this plan** explicitly, or **Change plan** to discard. Approval rechecks the preview fingerprint against current orders, quotes and availability.
4. **2:40–3:30 · Prepare.** The Prepare step shows a short approval summary and practical checklist instead of repeating the plan cards. Download **Download preparation manifest — NOT A BOOKING** for the five-row CSV. Optionally click **Mark fictional packages prepared**. This session marker is not a booking, payment, dispatch or tracking event. Real-world follow-up remains guidance: verify carrier rates and addresses, pack and label, then arrange real bookings elsewhere.
5. **3:30–4:25 · Handle a change.** Open **Something changed? · Demo controls and activity** and click **Simulate van unavailable**. The approval and preparation marker clear. The existing optimiser recalculates an all-parcel assumed plan of **RM132** and shows the prior **RM113** total, **RM19** increase and changed quote selections. A fresh review and human approval are required. **Restore van** also invalidates old approval.
6. **4:25–5:00 · Show a blocker and reset.** Use **View orders**, change FEST004's deadline to one day, and apply. The Plan step names FEST004 as lacking an available option. It shows no unsupported cost comparison and blocks approval and manifest. **Review deliveries** offers recovery. **Reset demo to orders** restores the original validated five orders and clears all plan, approval and history state except the new intake event.

**Before and after:** Previously the public demo opened on an empty invitation to load a scenario. Now the five sample orders are validated once per new browser session and visible immediately. Planning still requires a deliberate click; reruns do not create new intake events or calculate a plan on the Orders step. The scenario remains session-only and can be reset.

The workflow still uses the established validator, quote review, deterministic optimiser, fingerprinted preview/approval and manifest. The private Google OIDC/Render/Supabase pilot remains separate under **Private Pilot**; legacy tools are under **Advanced & other demos**. EasyParcel OAuth remains disabled. A solver-limited feasible result is never described as a proven optimum.
