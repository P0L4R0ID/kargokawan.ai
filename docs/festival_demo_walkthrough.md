# Festival Idea Putrajaya · five-minute fictional KargoKawan walkthrough

This is a **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION — NOT REAL CARRIER PRICES, QUOTES OR BOOKINGS**. It needs no CSV, courier login or API key. Use only synthetic data. The five-order flow lives in the browser's Streamlit session and never writes to authenticated PostgreSQL pilot jobs, quote drafts or approvals.

1. **0:00–0:50 · Intake and validation.** Open the Streamlit app, find **Festival challenge · five-order fictional walkthrough** near the top of the public fictional area, and click **Load five-order fictional scenario**. Show five orders from one Kuala Lumpur pickup: three distinct Klang Valley postcodes plus separate George Town and Johor Bahru parcels. Each has a fictional recipient, weight, dimensions, deadline and five-digit postcodes. The green intake message confirms the established CSV validator accepted them. No real customer details are used.
2. **0:50–1:50 · Alternatives and decision.** Show the alternatives table. It contains five complete fictional standalone parcel assumptions, one explicitly routed three-stop road-vehicle assumption for the three Klang Valley orders, and two tempting cheaper parcel assumptions that miss their deadlines. All quote IDs, providers, prices, transit times and route stops are labelled fictional. The deterministic exact-cover optimiser selects the three-stop vehicle plus two intercity parcels: **RM113.00 assumed total** versus a valid **RM132.00 all-parcel baseline**. The fictional RM19.00 difference is computed from the same eligible assumptions; it is not a real saving or provider quotation. Show the selected services, order groups, exclusion reasons and solver status.
3. **1:50–2:40 · Review and human approval.** Click **Preview five-order fictional plan**. Point out **PROPOSAL ONLY** and the absence of a manifest. Click **Approve five-order fictional plan** explicitly. The app rechecks the preview against current orders, quotes and provider availability. It never books or pays for a shipment.
4. **2:40–3:30 · Preparation.** Click **Download fictional preparation manifest — NOT A BOOKING**. Its five rows list fictional recipients, postcodes, deadlines, selected method, quote assumption ID and group total. Click **Mark fictional packages prepared** to show a session-only operational status; no dispatch or carrier tracking is claimed. The next-action message asks an operator to verify real addresses and rates, pack and label parcels, and request actual bookings outside this demonstration.
5. **3:30–4:30 · Disruption and replanning.** Click **Simulate fictional van disruption**. The earlier approval and preparation status clear. The same optimiser selects five standalone parcels for **RM132.00**, and **What changed** explains the RM19.00 increase and the replaced quote IDs. Preview and approve the replacement separately; no decision is silently applied. **Restore fictional van** returns to the original alternatives but still requires a fresh preview and approval.
6. **4:30–5:00 · Constraint honesty.** Select **FEST004** under **Fictional order to adjust deadline**, set **Revised fictional deadline (days)** to **1**, then click **Apply fictional deadline change**. No quoted alternative can meet that intercity deadline, so the flow says **NOT READY**, shows no valid all-parcel baseline, and prevents proposal approval or manifest export. The fictional decision history records the change.

The original four-order **Competition demo · mixed methods**, the public simulated dashboard, imported-quotation workflows, and the authenticated PostgreSQL pilot remain separate. The authenticated pilot continues to reject synthetic quotes. EasyParcel OAuth remains disabled and the workflow performs zero carrier calls. Rates, ETAs, capacity and multi-stop service are internally consistent *assumptions* solely for illustrating software behavior; actual availability and savings are unverified. The session-only demonstration resets when its browser session is lost. The UI uses the existing layout; a visual redesign is outside this phase.
