# Festival Idea Putrajaya · five-minute orders-first demo

The public workspace starts with **five fictional deliveries already loaded**. Prices and orders are fictional; there are no real bookings, carrier calls or pilot database writes. No login or CSV is needed.

1. **0:00–0:50 · Inspect the orders.** Open the app. Under **KargoKawan**, the Orders step says **You have 5 deliveries to arrange**. Destination, postcode, order ID and deadline appear on compact cards; pickup and full parcel details come from the validated scenario and are available in the details section. At this point there is **no selected delivery method or plan cost**. The detailed order evidence and deadline control are expandable.
2. **0:50–1:45 · Request planning.** Click **Find the best delivery plan**. This is the first time the fictional mixed-method optimiser runs. The Plan step leads with a dark assumed-cost summary and all-courier comparison, then presents a highlighted selected three-stop van and compact separate courier parcels for FEST004–005. Its calculated assumed total is **RM113**, with a valid **RM132** all-courier comparison and **RM19** difference. These figures are derived from the fictional quote dataset and solver, never real rates or guaranteed savings. Open **How was this calculated?** for quote IDs, rejected options, sources and solver status.
3. **1:45–2:40 · Review before approval.** Click **Review this delivery plan**. The Approve step shows a concise white decision surface with the amount, order coverage and group count; full groups are available only in **View full plan details**. The manifest is still unavailable. Click **Approve this plan** explicitly, or **Change plan** to discard. Approval rechecks the preview fingerprint against current orders, quotes and availability.
4. **2:40–3:30 · Prepare.** The Prepare step starts with **Prepare deliveries** and an approval summary and the manifest download. A single sentence explains the next real-world step. Open **Preparation details** for the full list of group-specific carrier, deadline, recipient-detail and packing tasks to mark internal checks or packing as *operator marked done*. These marks are session-only and do not verify a carrier. Download **Download preparation manifest — NOT A BOOKING** for the five-row CSV; the app does not claim the file was downloaded. **Mark fictional packages prepared** is a session marker, not a booking, payment, dispatch or tracking event. Real bookings remain outside the demo.
5. **3:30–4:25 · Handle a change.** Open **Something changed? · Demo controls and activity** and click **Simulate van unavailable**. The approval, preparation marker and proposal-tied task acknowledgements clear. The existing optimiser recalculates an all-parcel assumed plan of **RM132** and shows the prior **RM113** total, **RM19** increase and affected order methods. Technical option references are expandable. A fresh review and approval generate new group tasks. **Activity history** holds the fictional event trail. **Restore van** also invalidates old approval.
6. **4:25–5:00 · Show a blocker and reset.** Use **View orders**, change FEST004's deadline to one day, and apply. The Plan step names FEST004 as lacking an available option. It shows no unsupported cost comparison and blocks approval and manifest. **Review deliveries** offers recovery. **Reset demo to orders** restores the original validated five orders and clears all plan, approval and history state except the new intake event.

**Before and after:** Previously the public demo opened on an empty invitation to load a scenario. Now the five sample orders are validated once per new browser session and visible immediately. Planning still requires a deliberate click; reruns do not create new intake events or calculate a plan on the Orders step. The scenario remains session-only and can be reset.

The workflow still uses the established validator, quote review, deterministic optimiser, fingerprinted preview/approval and manifest. The private Google OIDC/Render/Supabase pilot remains separate under **Private Pilot**; legacy tools are under **Advanced & other demos**. EasyParcel OAuth remains disabled. A solver-limited feasible result is never described as a proven optimum.

The presentation patterns and visual-review limits are described in [product design](product_design.md).
